\paragraph{Status in this draft (v0).}
The study has been specified but not yet run. Its design is as follows. Six arms are evaluated with three seeds in the symmetric maze: \acp{} with branching, \acp{} without branching, \msp{} with branching, \msp{} without branching, the untrained recurrent encoder, and the history-only informative control. Each collection method gets a pretraining budget of 10{,}000 environment transitions per seed. The two branching arms share one dataset exactly, and so do the two no-branching arms. All six arms use the readout protocol above with a separate, shared, restore-free readout pool. The paired contrasts, the interaction, and the decision rules are listed in Appendix~\ref{app:planned}. No results of this study are reported in v0.
