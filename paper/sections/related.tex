\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Predictive state and history representations.}
PSRs represent state as predictions of action-conditional tests \citep{littman2001psr,singh2004psr}. Temporal-difference networks learn such predictions from the actions actually taken \citep{sutton2004tdnets}. Predictive-state decoders and PSRNNs attach future-observation prediction to recurrent networks; in the formulations we checked, the targets are not conditioned on future actions \citep{venkatraman2017psd,downey2017psrnn}, which makes them structurally closest to our action-free ablation. The approximate information state framework bounds the planning loss of a history compression that predicts rewards and its own next value given an action \citep{subramanian2022ais}. \citet{ni2024bridging} relate one-step self-predictive history objectives to that framework.

\paragraph{Action-conditional multi-step prediction.}
SimCore rolls a recurrent belief forward conditioned only on the agent's actions \citep{gregor2019simcore}. CPC$|$Action and PBL use action-conditional multi-step predictions from compressed histories \citep{guo2018belief,guo2020pbl}. \citet{guo2018belief} report that multi-step prediction and action conditioning are critical for their belief representations; our action-conditional versus action-free contrast is therefore not a new question. Multi-step latent prediction conditioned on actions also underlies SPR \citep{schwarzer2021spr}, TD-MPC2 \citep{hansen2024tdmpc2}, MuZero \citep{schrittwieser2020muzero}, and earlier action-conditional video prediction \citep{oh2015action}. Recurrent world models such as PlaNet and DreamerV3 learn latent dynamics in POMDPs \citep{hafner2019planet,hafner2023dreamerv3}. \citet{khetarpal2025unifying} compare per-action and policy-marginal self-prediction theoretically in MDPs. Task-oriented alternatives include bisimulation metrics \citep{zhang2021dbc}, value equivalence \citep{grimm2020ve}, and successor features \citep{dayan1993sr,barreto2017sf}.

\paragraph{Closest work.}
\citet{kwon2024future} train a history encoder with an action-conditional $k$-step observation predictor, decouple representation learning from reinforcement learning, and evaluate on memory tasks. Their test actions are inserted once per episode along the executed trajectory, without simulator branching. In the version we read, we did not find an action-free ablation. Our action-conditional variant is a small re-implementation in the same spirit, not an official reproduction of their method.

\paragraph{Resets, branching, and memory tasks.}
State restore for branching rollouts appears in TRPO's vine procedure \citep{schulman2015trpo}. Reset-and-replay is used to estimate PSR tests from a common history \citep{james2004reset}, and local simulator access has been analysed theoretically \citep{mhammedi2024resets}. In our deterministic task, reset-and-replay and save-and-restore are nearly equivalent. T-maze memory tasks and recurrent or world-model baselines for them are studied by \citet{ni2022recurrent}, \citet{ni2023transformers}, and \citet{samsami2024r2i}.
