## B) What is already known (component → established by)

- **History state as action-conditional multi-step predictions of future observations ("tests").** Littman, Sutton & Singh 2001; Singh, James & Rudary 2004. The float/reset example shows that no k-order Markov model suffices, which is observation aliasing. Our "action-conditional predictive representation" is PSR in concept and must be cited as such.
- **Learning action-conditional predictive questions from a recurrent or non-Markov state.** TD networks (Sutton & Tanner 2005), random deep action-conditional GVFs (Zheng et al. 2021), RPSP (Hefny et al. 2018). All train from executed actions.
- **RNN plus PSR-style future-prediction decoders.** PSD (Venkatraman et al. 2017) and PSRNN (Downey et al. 2017). Both predict future observations **without** action conditioning, which already matches the "action-marginal multi-step prediction" baseline.
- **Recurrent belief plus an action-conditional multi-step predictor as an auxiliary loss.** SimCore (Gregor et al. 2019), CPC\|Action (Guo et al. 2018), PBL (Guo et al. 2020). Guo et al. 2018 already report that action-conditioning and multi-step prediction matter for CPC-based belief learning.
- **History encoder plus action-conditional k-step observation prediction, framed as PSR, on memory POMDPs.** Kwon et al. 2024/2025 (R1).
- **Multi-step action-conditional latent consistency or planning models.** SPR (K=5, 4 stacked frames), TD-MPC2 (MDP, single-observation encoder, λ-weighted H-step joint-embedding loss), MuZero (observation history to hidden state, K-step unroll with real actions, reward/value/policy only), PlaNet/DreamerV3 (recurrent RSSM conditioned on actions, POMDP framing, reconstruction, latent overshooting). Oh et al. 2015 did action-conditional multi-step video prediction.
- **Action-conditional versus policy-marginal prediction.** Khetarpal et al. 2025 (BYOL-AC vs BYOL-Π; T_a vs T^π; MDP, one-step) in theory, and Guo et al. 2018 (CPC vs CPC\|Action) empirically. Reading the action-marginal predictor as a prediction of the policy-induced uncontrolled process is my interpretation of Singh et al. 2004's controlled/uncontrolled split; the papers do not state it.
- **History-based predictive objectives with planning or value bounds in POMDPs.** AIS (Subramanian et al. 2022): reward prediction plus next-AIS/observation prediction given the action, LSTM, approximate-DP bound. Ni et al. 2024: ZP/OP/RP for histories, one-step, deliberately without multi-step prediction or planning.
- **Task-oriented or decision-aware representations.** Should not be claimed. Covered by DBC (bisimulation), value equivalence, MuZero, successor features/SR, and AIS.
- **Simulator branching and state restore.** TRPO vine (multiple actions from restored states, common random numbers, explicitly more simulator calls). James & Singh 2004 (resets to estimate test outcomes for PSR learning). UFLP (Yin et al. 2023) and Mhammedi et al. 2024 (local simulator access).
- **T-maze memory tasks and perceptual aliasing.** Passive/Active T-Maze (Ni et al. 2023); earlier T-mazes (Bakker 2001, cited there; Lambrechts et al. 2022); memory benchmarks with world models (R2I; DreamerV3 on BSuite and DMLab); recurrent model-free baselines (Ni et al. 2022). Aliasing concept: Whitehead & Ballard 1991; McCallum 1996.

## C) Possibly not directly covered (not found in the papers checked; search not exhaustive)

- **Branching to supervise a neural history encoder.** Not found in the papers checked: save/restore of the exact ground-truth state at intermediate points to collect targets for several alternative action sequences, used to train a recurrent encoder's action-conditional multi-step predictor, with all branch transitions counted in the budget.
  - In the checked neural works, action-conditional predictors learn only from executed actions: TD nets, Zheng 2021, SimCore, PBL, CPC\|Action, SPR, MuZero, TD-MPC2, R2I. Kwon et al. insert one random test action per episode along the trajectory.
  - The branching precedents serve other purposes: TRPO vine (advantage estimation), UFLP and Mhammedi et al. (exploration and planning), James & Singh 2004 (tabular linear-PSR learning, reset to the null history).
  - **Caveat:** in a deterministic T-maze, James & Singh-style "reset plus replay of the history's actions" is operationally close to save/restore. Any difference claimed should be narrow.
- **A matched comparison in an aliased T-maze with planning evaluation.** Not found in the papers checked: action-conditional versus action-marginal multi-step prediction with an identical recurrent encoder, predictor and data, on a T-maze-like aliased task, evaluated by planning with the learned model. The closest are Guo et al. 2018 (DMLab, belief decoding, contrastive), Khetarpal et al. 2025 (MDP, one-step, theory plus deep RL) and Kwon et al. 2024/25 (action-conditional only, no action-marginal ablation found in the fetched sections).
- **Planning with a history-conditioned action-conditional predictor on Passive/Active T-Maze.** Not found in the papers checked. Ni et al. 2023 evaluate model-free agents there. The planning agents checked (PlaNet, DreamerV3, MuZero, TD-MPC2, R2I) are not reported on these T-mazes in the sections read.
